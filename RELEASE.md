# Version 1.0.0 — storefront source release

Includes all storefront pages, illustrative product catalogue, product detail views, size and quantity selection, browser-persisted shopping bag, AED totals, preview promo and shipping options, validated checkout form, clearly labelled preview confirmation, and email inquiry drafts.

The server-only Stripe template and README describe live integration and deployment. No keys are embedded.

## Launch requirements
- Replace sample products, images, prices, sizes and shipping rates.
- Confirm business information, taxes and final policies.
- Provide the business WhatsApp number.
- Deploy payment endpoints, durable order storage, verified webhooks and email delivery.
- Verify mobile/desktop layout and payment sandbox scenarios.

This is the final source release of the storefront preview. It is not yet an operational shop accepting paid orders.
